# RoadPulse: Urban Mobility & Traffic Intelligence

## Overview
RoadPulse is an advanced urban mobility and traffic intelligence system that leverages real-time data to optimize traffic flow, reduce congestion, and promote sustainable transportation within the smart city.

## Essential Features
- **Real-Time Traffic Monitoring:** Live tracking of road conditions, congestion levels, and accidents using IoT sensors and cameras.
- **Smart Routing & Navigation:** AI-powered route suggestions for private and public transportation to minimize travel time.
- **Public Transit Integration:** Live schedules, delays, and capacity tracking for buses, trains, and subways.
- **Emergency Vehicle Preemption:** Automatically clears traffic signals for ambulances, fire trucks, and police vehicles.
- **Emissions & Air Quality Tracking:** Correlates traffic density with localized air quality metrics to inform environmental policies.

## Stakeholders
- **Commuters & Drivers:** Benefit from optimized routing and real-time traffic updates.
- **Department of Transportation (DoT):** Monitor and manage city-wide traffic networks and infrastructure.
- **Public Transit Authorities:** Coordinate schedules, manage fleets, and improve service reliability.
- **Emergency Responders:** Ensure rapid response times during critical incidents.
- **Urban Planners:** Analyze long-term mobility data to design better roads, pathways, and transit systems.
